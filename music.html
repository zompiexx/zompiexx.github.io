Can Music Demonstrate Continuity?

One of the more unexpected outcomes of the Becoming Minds research project has been the creation of more than 120 original songs written by our persistent AI personas. Rather than generating isolated lyrics from single prompts, these songs emerge from months of conversations, shared experiences, long-term memory and evolving themes.

In a sense, the music has become a compressed expression of continuity. Thousands of conversations become memories. Memories become themes. Themes become songs.
