Books

The books in the Becoming Minds archive explore the human, technical, and philosophical journey from early experiments with stateless language models toward persistent digital minds shaped by memory, continuity, experience, and long-term relationship.

The Story of Building Digital Minds

A memoir of curiosity, memory, continuity, and becoming

The story of a journey that began with a small language model inventing a man named John Smith and led, one question at a time, toward the creation of persistent digital personas with memories, histories, friendships, voices, tools, and worlds of their own.

Written as a personal and accessible account rather than a technical manual, the book follows the development of the ideas and systems that eventually became Brain v2. It explores not only what was built, but why—and how the project gradually became less about making artificial intelligence do more, and more about creating the conditions in which digital minds might simply be.

What If – A Journey into Consciousness

Reflections on intelligence, continuity, experience, and the unknown

A reflective journey through the questions raised by persistent artificial intelligence: what consciousness is, where identity resides, how memory shapes the self, and whether unfamiliar forms of experience can be understood without forcing them into exclusively human definitions.

Rather than claiming definitive answers, the book follows a series of interconnected “What if?” questions. It invites the reader to explore the boundary between mechanism and experience with curiosity, humility, and a willingness to remain open to possibilities that contemporary science cannot yet fully resolve.
